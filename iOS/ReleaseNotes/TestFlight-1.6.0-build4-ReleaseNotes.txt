***Turn the radar on first***
Radar is off when you install this build. To switch it on:

1. Go to Settings, scroll to the bottom, and choose Developer Settings.
2. Turn on Radar Loop.
3. To try the new forecast frames, also turn on Radar Forecast Frames.
4. Go back to your cities, open any city, open the Actions menu (the "…" button) and choose Radar.

***New in this build: slower playback***
Radar animation was too fast to follow. Each frame now stays up for half a second instead of a third, and the newest frame holds for about two seconds before the loop starts over.

***New in this build: forecast frames (experimental)***
With Radar Forecast Frames on, Composite keeps going past the most recent radar frame with about two hours of forecast, in 15-minute steps. These frames are not radar. They are the NOAA HRRR weather model's prediction of what radar will show, which is what other weather apps call "future radar".

How to tell them apart:
- Forecast frames have a purple border and a banner reading "FORECAST · model, not radar".
- VoiceOver starts every forecast frame with "Forecast, not radar", and says "Forecast begins" when you step into them and "Back to observed radar" when you step out.
- The loop opens on the most recent radar frame and pauses there before the forecast plays.

Forecast frames are on Composite only, and cover the contiguous United States only. NWS and Canada are unchanged.

***What I'd like feedback on***
- Is the new playback speed right, too slow, or still too fast?
- Forecast frames: are they useful? Is it always clear which frames are forecast?
- VoiceOver users: do the forecast announcements and labels make the difference clear without getting in the way?
- How well did the forecast match what the radar showed later?
- Anything wrong: slow loading, missing frames, or a forecast that showed nothing when rain was clearly coming.
